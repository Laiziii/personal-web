About me

I started at Brock in September 2023 in Public Health. Since then my studies have moved through health sciences and the social sciences more broadly, but public health is still the thing I keep coming back to.

What gets me is the bigger picture: why some communities are healthier than others, how the places we live and the lives we lead shape our health, and what actually works to prevent disease before it starts.

Outside of class I’m usually doing something on a computer. Tweaking my PC, trying a new AI tool, playing Fortnite, or reading way too much about the stock market. Those interests look unrelated, but most of them come back to the same question: how does this work, and could it work better?

I’m still figuring out exactly where all of this leads, and I’m fine with that. This site is a snapshot of where I am right now. It’ll change as I do.
